# Candidate local models for ~8 GB VRAM (RTX 4060 Laptop 8 GB in this setup).
# One model per line. `#` starts a comment. Pull them yourself or use --pull.
#
# Rule of thumb at Q4: 3-4B models fit with room for context; 7-8B models fit
# tightly (~5 GB) and leave little VRAM for long contexts; 12B+ spill to CPU.
# Speed on this GPU: roughly 250-350 tok/s (0.1-1B), 60-100 (3-4B),
# 30-50 (7-8B) for decode, before any context or thermal throttling.
